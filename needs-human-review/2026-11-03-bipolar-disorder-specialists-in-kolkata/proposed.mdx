## What Does a Bipolar Disorder Specialist Do?

A bipolar disorder specialist carries out a thorough psychiatric assessment to confirm diagnosis and distinguish [bipolar disorder](/illnesses/bipolar-disorder) from depression alone, since the two are treated very differently. Treatment is built around mood-stabilising medication, with therapy — often [CBT](/treatments/cognitive-behavioural-therapy-cbt) or family-focused approaches — used to support sleep routine, stress management and recognition of early warning signs of an episode.
