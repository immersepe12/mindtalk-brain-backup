## Which Type of Psychotherapy Do You Need?

| Approach | Best For | Typical Session Count |
|---|---|---|
| [CBT](/treatments/cognitive-behavioural-therapy-cbt) | Anxiety, depression, unhelpful thought patterns | 8–16 sessions |
| [DBT](/treatments/dialectical-behaviour-therapy-dbt) | Emotional regulation, personality-level difficulties | 6 months or longer, often in modules |
| EMDR | Trauma and PTSD | 6–12 sessions |
| ACT | Chronic stress, avoidance patterns, values-based change | 8–12 sessions |
| Psychodynamic / talk therapy | Longstanding patterns, relationship and identity concerns | Open-ended, often longer-term |

Your psychotherapist will usually recommend an approach after your first session, once they understand what you're dealing with — you don't need to know which one you need before you book.

## Psychotherapist vs Psychologist vs Psychiatrist: Who Should You See?

A psychotherapist delivers structured talk therapy — this can be a clinical psychologist, counselling psychologist, or trained therapist. A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who can prescribe medication; many clients see a psychiatrist and a psychotherapist together, with the psychiatrist managing medication and the psychotherapist providing ongoing therapy. If you're unsure which you need, a Mindtalk care coordinator can guide you — see our [explainer on psychologists vs psychiatrists](/doctors/psychiatrists-in-bangalore) for more detail, or [read about psychotherapy as a treatment](/treatments/psychotherapy).

## What Happens in Your First Psychotherapy Session?

Your first session focuses on understanding what has brought you in, relevant history, and what you're hoping to get from therapy. Your psychotherapist will usually propose an approach and a rough plan by the end of the first or second session, including how often you'll meet. Sessions typically run 45–60 minutes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I choose the right psychotherapist in Bangalore?
Start with what you're dealing with — some modalities (like EMDR for trauma, or ERP for OCD) are a closer match for specific concerns than others. Beyond that, fit with your therapist matters as much as their approach. Most people know within the first session or two whether a particular psychotherapist feels right for them.

### What qualifications should a psychotherapist in India have?
Look for RCI (Rehabilitation Council of India) registration for clinical psychologists, or a recognised postgraduate qualification in counselling or clinical psychology. All of Mindtalk's psychotherapists are formally trained and registered where applicable — check each profile for specific qualifications.

### How many psychotherapy sessions will I need?
This depends on the modality and what you're working on. Many people notice meaningful change within 8–12 sessions for mild to moderate anxiety or depression; trauma-focused or personality-level work can take longer. Your psychotherapist will discuss a realistic plan at your first session.
